The house

Your place.
To stay, to live,
to inspire, to be.

Nestled in the beautiful city of Kalamata, our apartments combine timeless elegance with modern comfort to provide you with a truly exceptional stay. From the moment you step through our doors, you’ll be enveloped in an atmosphere of refined urban luxury and impeccable hospitality.

Allofts offers a range of meticulously designed lofts and suites, each exuding its own distinct charm and character.

Whether you’re seeking a tranquil retreat for a romantic getaway or a spacious haven for a family holiday, our accommodations offer the perfect blend of style and functionality. With our attention to detail, we strive to provide a space you’ll be proud to call home.

We look forward to welcoming you to Allofts and ensuring your stay with us is nothing short of extraordinary. Let us be your home away from home, where luxury meets hospitality in perfect harmony.

Contemporary Living

Four ways to feel at home

  • 01

    To stay

    Eight lofts, suites and rooms from 28 to 56 m², each designed down to the last detail.

  • 02

    To live

    Equipped kitchens, living areas and room to settle in — for a weekend or for longer.

  • 03

    To inspire

    A mural, a suspended net, a crystal chandelier, hand-picked furniture: no two spaces are alike.

  • 04

    To be

    A roof garden, a gym and hosts who are there whenever you need them.

On the roof

Above it all, a roof garden

A timber deck, a long table and deep loungers under the open sky, with the city below and the mountains beyond. Come up for a coffee in the morning light, or a glass of wine as the sun goes down.

Fitness

And a gym in the house

Keep your routine while you travel: an in-house fitness room with a treadmill, spin bikes and a multi-gym station.

The building

White volumes, concrete and light

Clean white volumes, exposed concrete and black steel by day; a soft play of light once the sun has set. A discreet entrance, a sculptural staircase and a lift lead to each door.
