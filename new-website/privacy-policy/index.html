Privacy

This website is a simple brochure for Allofts, built to respect your privacy.

No cookies, no tracking

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers on this website. Fonts and images are served by the website itself, not by third parties.

Enquiry form

The form on the Contact page does not send anything through the website. It prepares an email in your own email app, and nothing is sent until you press send there. When you email or call us, we use your details only to answer your request and to manage your reservation.

Map

The map is provided by Google and loads only when you ask for it. From that moment, Google’s own privacy policy applies.

Booking

When you choose “Book now” you leave this website for Booking.com, where their terms and privacy policy apply.

Questions

For anything concerning your personal data, write to us at info@allofts.gr.
