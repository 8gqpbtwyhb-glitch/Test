About this space

Welcome to our exquisite Executive Loft I, a spacious and luxurious retreat designed to accommodate up to four people. This exceptional 55 sq. m. loft offers an expansive living space and a fully equipped kitchen that seamlessly blends style, comfort and functionality, ensuring an unforgettable stay for you and your companions.

Our modern staircase leads you to a cocooning bedroom with an extra-comfortable queen-sized bed, ensuring a restful night’s sleep.
